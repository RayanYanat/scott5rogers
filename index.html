About

A career built on the pitch — and recognised beyond it.

Scott Rogers BEM is the Head of Technical Coaching at the England Amputee Football Association, and formerly Head Coach of the England national squad. His work spans elite tournament preparation, international competition, and the technical development of players representing their country.

Awarded the British Empire Medal in recognition of his services to the sport, Scott has guided England through UEFA European Championships, international training camps, and Nations League competition — while raising the profile of amputee football through national media appearances.

The Journey

Tournaments, camps & campaigns

A look back at the fixtures, camps and championships shaping England's amputee football programme.

  1. 2021

    Training Camps 2021

    A season of intensive preparation cycles with the England squad, building fitness, tactics and squad cohesion ahead of tournament football.

  2. 2021

    UEFA Euro 2021 — Poland

    Coached England at the European Championships, based in Kraków, competing against the continent's leading amputee football nations.

  3. 2022

    Training Camp — January 2022

    A new-year training camp focused on technical development and squad depth ahead of the season's fixtures.

  4. 2023

    Nations League 2023

    International fixtures and continued squad development through the 2023 Nations League campaign.

  5. 2024

    Turkey Training Camp

    A warm-weather training camp in Turkey, sharpening the squad's conditioning and tactical shape.

  6. 2024

    UEFA Euro 2024 — France

    Led technical coaching for England at the European Championships in France.
